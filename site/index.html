      What we sell

      A finished serum for skin, and the powder for people who formulate.

      The serum

      A water-based cosmetic for the look of fine lines and the feel of moisturized skin. Apply a few drops to clean, intact skin, morning or night. Patch-test first. Keep out of reach of children.

      The powder

      GHK-Cu by net weight, a cosmetic ingredient for formulators. Not a finished serum and not a vial. No mix rate for skin, and no instruction for swallowing or injecting. Confirm the formulator notice before checkout.
