Ingredient research

What the GHK-Cu studies measured

GHK-Cu is glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine bound to copper. On a cosmetic label the INCI name is Copper Tripeptide-1.

A famous early paper (Maquart et al., 1988) reported more collagen synthesis in cultured fibroblasts. That sentence is about cells in a dish. A later human study people cite is small, set after laser resurfacing, and mixed: satisfaction moved; several blinded measurements did not. We keep those results in their own rooms.

If you want the serum, it is here for appearance and feel. If you want the powder, it is a cosmetic ingredient for formulators, sold separately, with its own notice.

How we read a result.

ClassHow we read it
LaboratoryMeasured in cells. Useful for a mechanism question. Not a result on a face.
AnimalMeasured in an animal model. It does not transfer to people on its own.
HumanMeasured in people. We say how many, what was compared, and whether anyone was blinded.
ReviewA summary of earlier papers. Only as strong as those papers.
This bottleWhat we claim for the serum is appearance and feel. The papers are linked beside it, labeled as papers.

We have not run a finished-product test on this serum. When we do, it gets its own line here, separate from the ingredient research above. A paper next to a buy button would still be a paper. It does not become what the serum does.
